1. Download & Install the App

One-Time
Android Phones (Google Chrome)
  1. Open erp.nitp.ac.in/safeexit in Chrome (not WhatsApp/in-app).
  2. Tap the bottom banner Install SafeExit.
  3. Or tap Chrome menu (⋮) → tap Install app / Add to Home screen.
  4. Confirm Install. Launch SafeExit from your home screen icon.
iPhone / iPad (Apple Safari)
  1. Open erp.nitp.ac.in/safeexit in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share icon (square with upward arrow).
  3. Scroll down and select Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add in the top-right. Open SafeExit like any iOS app.
Laptops & PCs (Chrome / MS Edge)
  1. Open the portal link in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the Install icon in the address bar (right corner).
  3. Click Install to add SafeExit as a desktop application.

2. Sign-In & Authentication

Security
  1. Launch the installed app, or open /login/warden.
  2. Tap Sign in with Google.
  3. Select your official Institute email (@nitp.ac.in).
  4. Zero Password Hassle: No passwords, IDs, or PINs to remember or risk sharing. Access is strictly tied to verified college credentials.
  5. Auto-Hostel Assignment: The system automatically binds you to your designated hostel (Aryabhatta, Brahmaputra, Kosi, Ganga, Bagmati).
Access Note: Only accounts pre-approved by the Chief Warden / Admin can log in. If you see "Not registered as warden", contact the hostel administrator.

3. First-Time Setup Checklist

Required
  • Upload / Draw Digital Signature: Go to Profile or Leave Desk → draw or upload your signature once. Your signature is permanently stamped on approved leave gate passes.
  • Enable Push Notifications: Tap "Enable" when prompted. Allows immediate sound and notification alerts for new leave requests & emergencies even when the app is closed.

4. Daily Operational Workflow & Responsibilities

Daily Desk

Review Leave Applications (Leave Desk)

Girls' Hostels: Every leave request arrives concurrently on the Warden, Asst. Warden, and Caretaker queues. The first officer to act decides. Verify student face photo, roll number, leave duration, reason, destination, and parent contact.

Boys' Hostels: Leave requests are auto-approved per institute policy. Wardens hold full authority to review, audit, or Revoke approval at any point.

Approve or Reject with 1 Tap

Tap Approve — your stored digital signature is cryptographically affixed to the pass, and the student's gate exit QR code is generated instantly. If rejecting, provide a clear brief reason.

Local Daily Outings (Curfew Rules)

Routine daily outings do not require manual warden approval. Students scan out and in at the main gate scanner. Outings are automatically tracked against evening curfew hours.

Emergency SOS Alerts

High-priority visual & audio siren for any student in distress. Displays student identity, room number, exact timestamp, and 1-tap phone dials to student & parent.

Overdue Tracking

Instantly lists students who missed their gate curfew or leave return deadline. One-tap direct call to student and guardian for prompt follow-up.

Student Delay Notices

Review advance late-arrival submissions filed by students prior to curfew deadline explaining transit delays with supporting details.

Live Movement Logs

24/7 searchable real-time audit trail of all student gate entries and exits scoped exclusively to your assigned hostel.

Hostel Safety Rule: Treat all Active SOS alerts and Overdue flags as immediate priority. Coordinate with gate security and the hostel caretaker for rapid physical checks.

5. Quick Troubleshooting & Guidelines

Support
  • Multiple Wardens: Decisions sync live across all wardens and caretakers of your hostel. Once decided, the entry clears from other officers' queues.
  • Offline / Slow Network: The PWA caches offline data and will automatically synchronize pending actions when network connectivity resumes.
  • Signature Changes: To update your stored signature, visit the Profile tab and tap Edit Signature.
