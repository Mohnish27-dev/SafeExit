NITP-SafeExit Student Setup Guide

Download, install, and register in a few minutes.

SE
Official app address: erp.nitp.ac.in/safeexit
The app is a PWA. Do not download an APK or search for it in an app store.

1. Download and install

Android - use Google Chrome

  1. Open the official address in Chrome, not inside WhatsApp, Instagram, or another in-app browser.
  2. Tap the on-screen Install button.
  3. If it does not appear, tap Chrome's three-dot menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Confirm Install, then open NITP-SafeExit from the new home-screen icon.

iPhone / iPad - use Safari

  1. Open the official address in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (the square with an upward arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Keep the name NITP-SafeExit, tap Add, and open it from the home-screen icon.
Security: Install only from the official hostel link. Never install an APK or use an unofficial forwarded link.

2. Keep these ready

  • NITP email and inbox access
  • Roll number, branch, and year
  • Hostel and room number
  • Your and guardian's phone numbers
  • Clear recent face photo
  • One or two trusted contacts

3. Register your student account

Open registration

Open the installed app, choose Student Login, and tap Create an account.

Enter your details

Enter your full name, @nitp.ac.in email, roll number, branch, year, hostel, room number, mobile number, guardian number, and a password of at least 6 characters. Your mobile and guardian numbers must be different.

Verify your college email

Open your NITP mailbox, find the 6-digit verification code, and enter it in the app. Check Spam or Promotions if it is missing; use Resend after the timer ends.

Add your photo and signature

Upload a clear, front-facing photo of yourself. Crop it so your face is easy to identify. Then draw your signature on screen or upload a clear signature image.

Add trusted contacts

Add one or two roommates or close friends. Enter each person's name, mobile number, and room number. Their phone number must be different from yours. Ph.D. students can also provide their faculty details, and enter their faculty cabin number for the room number.

Set up quick login and finish

Create and confirm a 4-digit PIN. Enable fingerprint or Face ID if available, then finish setup. The Student Dashboard confirms that registration is complete.

Important: Use your real institute details. Your photo identifies you at the gate, and your saved signature is attached to outing and leave requests. Never share your password, PIN, or QR code.
NITP-SafeExit - Student setup only. For account or installation problems, contact the hostel office or caretaker.
